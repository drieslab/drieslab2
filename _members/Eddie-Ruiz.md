Edward C. (Eddie) Ruiz is a Postdoctoral Fellow in the Dries lab. Previously,
he was a [Hariri Institute Graduate Student
Fellow](https://www.bu.edu/hic/profile/edward-ruiz/) and *Ph.D.* candidate in
the Genetics and Genomics program. His research interests include spatial
omics, RNA processing, and machine learning. Outside of lab, Eddie enjoys long
walks on the esplanade and learning new programming languages.
