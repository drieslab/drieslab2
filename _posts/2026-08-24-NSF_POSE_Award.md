We are thrilled to announce that the Dries Lab has received an **NSF POSE (Pathways to Enable Open-Source Ecosystems) award** to transition Giotto Suite into a sustainable, community-driven open-source platform for spatial multi-scale and multi-omics data science.

The project will audit the current ecosystem, consult with our community of users and developers, and synthesize a technical charter and governance structure — working toward shared standards that make spatial omics tools more accessible across academic, clinical, and industry settings.

This work is a collaboration with co-PI Guo-Cheng Yuan (Icahn School of Medicine at Mount Sinai).
